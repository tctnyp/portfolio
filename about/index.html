About Me

My story so far, written like a living blog.

I'm Tan Chee Tiong. This page is where I keep the story of how I got into programming, server hosting, and Minecraft development, and where I document the kind of builder I am becoming. I like systems that feel calm, useful, and easy to trust.

I want this space to read like a real set of paragraphs, not a wall of boxes. That makes it easier for me to keep writing, revising, and adding new chapters as my story changes.

Chapter One

Curiosity came first.

I started by wanting to understand how things worked behind the screen. That curiosity grew into a habit of experimenting, breaking things, fixing them, and learning the shape of the system underneath. Over time, that habit turned into a genuine love for building.

The more I learned, the more I wanted to keep going. Each small discovery made the next one feel more reachable, and that is still how I like to learn now.

Chapter Two

The server side taught me discipline.

Server hosting pushed me to think about reliability, uptime, maintenance, and the quiet details people only notice when something fails. It taught me to design for stability, not just for the first successful launch.

That mindset changed how I look at every project. I now care a lot about whether something stays understandable after I come back to it later, not just whether it works once.

Chapter Three

Building with purpose.

I focus on code and infrastructure that are understandable, maintainable, and useful for real people. That includes keeping workflows clear, reducing unnecessary complexity, and making sure the final result feels calm to use.

I'm especially interested in the overlap between programming, hosting, and Minecraft development, because that combination lets me build systems that are both technical and practical.
