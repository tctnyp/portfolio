Intro

Hey everyone, I'm Chee Tiong, and this is the my blog of how I got into programming and hosting.

This blog will talk about Eaglercraft, Minecraft and Device Jailbreaking which got me into programming and hosting.

2022 — Secondary 1

In Secondary 1 we were given school chromebooks for our studies. It came bundled with a device management which I hated as it restricted many important sites that I used and I became pissed off with it. This was also the year when I started seeing classmates play "eaglercraft" in school.

If you didn't know, Eaglercraft is an AOT compiled voxel game inspired from Minecraft designed to run on Javascript. It uses TeaVM and LAX1DUDE's OpenGL emulator to run a Java virtual machine fully compatible with browsers. Eaglercraft can be played on ChromeOS, iOS, Android, and pretty much any device with a web browser; including your smart fridge.

As such, I started researching ways to disable the extension to play this game with my friends. It was also around this time when I decided to start developing my own Minecraft Server as playing in a private server could sometimes be more fun than playing in a public server.

2023 — Secondary 2/h2>

During this time, most of the "hacks" I used to bypass the device management were patched and as such I started looking for alternative ways. I eventually stumbled upon SH1MMER which was a tool that allowed me to unenroll my chromebook from the school.

Obviously, unenrolling my chromebook got me into some serious consequences but the chromebook was never "fixed" and I was left with a jailbroken chromebook which allowed me to do whatever I wanted. As such I started focusing slightly more on my minecraft server and was interested in making my own plugins.

2024 — Secondary 3

Whilst looking for a place to host my minecraft server, I stumbled upon Zenix.SG and became friends with the owner. I learned most of my programming, server management and ai skills from him.

Eventually, I realised that hosting was a much better way to make money compared to minecraft servers as you had to maintain the server daily. However, with hosting, you could just set up the server, rent it and cash out. It was a great way to earn passive income. I also started getting interested in the server hosting industry and learnt about the different Operating Systems, System Specifications e.t.c.

After learning about server optimizations, I immediately started thinking of ways to optimise my chromebook because it was still a pile of useless scrap. As such, I took my chromebook and replaced ChromeOS with Ubuntu 24 LTS. This gave me many more customization options and performance boosts. For the 1st time, I was able to achieve 300fps in a minecraft server on my chromebook.

2025 — Secondary 4

After quitting eaglercraft ince secondary 2, I decided to return back to Eaglercraft and became Co-Owner of Hyper Studios around Late October. I then became helped develop and maintain the server, though I was mostly focused on remaking the staff team.

At the same time I also started making Discord Bots because I had to use so many different bots for my server even though all of the claimed to be "All-in-One" bots. The end result was HyperXUtility which combined 6+ bots into a singular bot.

I also became a support staff for Zenix.SG and assisted their server hosting team.

Meanwhile I also had to deal with O-levels which was that year. Overall, it was a very busy year for me and I had to juggle between my studies, server hosting and Eaglercraft.

Now — Present

As of this moment, I am currently studying Common Business Technology (CBTP) at Nanyang Polytechnic so that I can use it for my hobbies. I believe that the Business and Engineering aspect can help Zenix.SG improve while the Technology aspect can help improve Hyper Network.
