About Me

My story so far, written like a living blog.

I'm Tan Chee Tiong. This page is where I keep the story of how I got into programming, server hosting, and Minecraft development, and where I document the kind of builder I am becoming. I like systems that feel calm, useful, and easy to trust.

Chapter One

Curiosity came first.

I started by wanting to understand how things worked behind the screen. That curiosity grew into a habit of experimenting, breaking things, fixing them, and learning the shape of the system underneath. Over time, that habit turned into a genuine love for building.

Chapter Two

The server side taught me discipline.

Server hosting pushed me to think about reliability, uptime, maintenance, and the quiet details people only notice when something fails. It taught me to design for stability, not just for the first successful launch.

At a glance

  • Interested in dependable hosting and clear workflows
  • Enjoy building Minecraft tools and community experiences
